A new home for family health in Bryanston

Luminos Pathways is a multi-disciplinary centre for families and young children. Psychologists, therapists and doctors will work side by side under one roof, so families can find the right support in one welcoming place.

Illuminating pathways to mental and physical wellbeing

About the centre

Care that sees the whole family

Health is rarely a single appointment. At Luminos Pathways, practitioners from different disciplines share one address, know each other's work and can refer to one another easily. Families spend less time driving across town and more time getting the help they came for.

Luminos Pathways is nestled within the Baby and Child Wellness Centre, home to many other supportive practitioners and services for families.

All under one roof

Psychology, therapy and medical care in one place, with easy referrals between practitioners.

A calm place to wait

A warm reception area with a coffee shop, a kids corner and a garden.

Friendly support

A full-time receptionist to help with bookings, payments and any questions you have.

Practise with us

Rooms available for practitioners

We are looking for warm, collaborative practitioners who enjoy working as part of a team. Full-time rooms come with:

  • A spacious, unfurnished consulting room
  • A full-time receptionist using GoodX for bookings, invoices and payments
  • In-house referrals across disciplines
  • Marketing support for your practice
  • A patient-friendly reception area with a coffee shop on site
  • Wi-Fi, a garden and a professional setting for your clients
